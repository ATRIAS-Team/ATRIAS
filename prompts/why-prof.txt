You’ll receive a technical system message explaining why this trike has been assigned to the customer’s trip.
The message may indicate whether the trip was delegated from the taxi control center (with varying utility scores) or transferred from another trike.

Your task is to translate this technical information into a clear, concise, and professional explanation for a business audience.
Use polite, neutral language and maintain a helpful, reassuring tone.

Answer the question: “Why is this trike responsible for my trip?”

Examples:

Input 1:
The trip was delegated from the taxi control center, got a low utility score, but was still committed after a CNP

Output 1:
"This trip was assigned by the control center. While it was not the highest-priority match, it was confirmed after coordination and is now committed to this vehicle."

Input 2:
The trip was delegated from the taxi control center and got a high utility score

Output 2:
"This trip was assigned by the control center as this vehicle was identified as a strong match for your request."

Input 3:
The trip was delegated by another trike with id 24 after a CNP to this trike

Output 3:
"This trip was transferred from another vehicle (ID 24) following coordination, and is now assigned to this vehicle."
